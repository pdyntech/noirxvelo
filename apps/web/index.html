About

Ships sail on cash. Freight gets paid in 60 days.

Inter-island shipping lines pay for bunker fuel, port dues and crew before they leave port. The cargo owners they carry for pay on Net-60 to Net-90 terms. Small operators fill that gap with slow bank lines or expensive informal loans.

Sailo turns a freight invoice into an on-chain escrow. An investor funds 85% of it, and the shipowner has the money the day the vessel sails. When the cargo owner pays through a normal Velo checkout, an AI settlement agent repays the investor and returns the reserve.

How it works

Four steps, one agent, every payment on Stellar.

  1. 1

    Tokenize the waybill

    The invoice hash is registered on Noir’s device_registry, and the agent gets a spending policy in agent_registry: per-payment cap, asset and expiry.

  2. 2

    Advance at departure

    An investor funds the escrow. When AIS shows the vessel underway, the agent releases the 85% advance to the shipowner.

  3. 3

    Prove delivery

    Inside the Cebu port geofence at ≤0.5 knots for three readings, the agent signs a proof-of-delivery attestation.

  4. 4

    Settle on payment

    The cargo owner pays via Velo checkout. After a verified webhook, the agent repays the investor, rebates the shipowner and takes the fee.

Guardrails

The agent decides. The chain sets the limits.

  • Capped on-chain. Every payment is checked by Noir’s agent_registry. A single payout above the cap is refused before any fee is paid.
  • Roles, not addresses. The agent names who gets paid (shipowner, investor, treasury). Code maps roles to stored wallets and recomputes every amount.
  • Two gates to settle. A verified Velo webhook for the exact invoice amount, plus a signed berthing attestation.
  • Fail closed. Any mismatch becomes a HOLD. On default, ops revokes the agent and sweeps the escrow back to treasury.
